Before you ask

FAQs

Why does Q need my SMS?+

Q reads bank transaction alerts automatically, so you never log an expense by hand. Granting access is required for the app to work; there's no manual-entry fallback if you say no.

Is my data actually private?+

Only messages from verified bank sender IDs get read. Personal texts and OTPs are never touched. The AI Coach runs its analysis on your phone, so your spending questions don't have to leave it to get answered.

Why Android only?+

Automatic SMS reading depends on an Android-specific permission that iOS doesn't expose to any app. iOS support would need a different, bank-integration-based approach, which isn't built yet.

What happens after the free trial?+

You're billed ₹99/month (or ₹999/year on the annual plan) starting day 8, cancellable any time from your phone's subscription settings. No charge happens without you actively starting the trial first.
